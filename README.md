# Federico Block

**Technology, media and marketing since 1994. From SGI in television to voice and LLMs. Two exits.**

Founder of [Mindgeist](https://mindgeist.com) · Madrid · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/federicoblock/)

Innovation and strategy consultant. I help leadership teams decide where AI and new technology change the business, and I build enough of it myself to know what works. Founder of two acquired agencies, former CTO and head of strategy, mentor to startups.

| **Entrepreneurship** | Founder of a digital agency and a mobile marketing agency, both acquired. Founder of Mindgeist since 2008. |
| **Mentorship** | Mentor at Madrid Emprende's mentor network. Team enablement as part of every engagement. |

**Sectors:** media · marketing, advertising and communication · technology.

## Background

- **Mindgeist** · Founder · 2008–present
  AI and voice strategy, phygital experiences (retail, digital signage, computer vision) and digital transformation for brands, agencies and institutions. Top publisher by volume of Spanish-language Alexa skills (Spain, Mexico and Hispanic US).
- **By Cycle** · CTO, strategy and creative
  Mobile marketing agency. Acquired by Buongiorno Vitaminic (2007).
- **Tsubcero** · Founder and director
  Digital agency. Acquired by WPP/Ogilvy (2001).
- **Artear (TV)**
  Production digitization, real-time virtual actors for the 1994 World Cup, launch of the Interactive Media unit. Silicon Graphics advisory board. Delegate at the MIT Media Lab, in the *News in the Future* and *Television of Tomorrow* programs.

## In progress

- **BLK Assistant**: modular braille learning system with a voice assistant.
- [Dimoria](https://dimoria.homes): guest guides for short-term rental hosts.

## Open source

Tools spun out of larger projects:

- [pi-here](https://github.com/fedeblock/pi-here): per-folder launcher for the Pi coding agent, with Ollama.
- [timecode-calc](https://github.com/fedeblock/timecode-calc): SMPTE timecode calculator, web and desktop.
- [mqttaudio-mindgeist](https://github.com/fedeblock/mqttaudio-mindgeist): MQTT-controlled audio player, adapted for BLK Assistant (fork of mqttaudio).
